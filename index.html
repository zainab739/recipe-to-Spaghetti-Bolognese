Recipe Details

Spaghetti Bolognese

Ingredients:

  • Spaghetti
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Garlic and onion

Instructions:

  1. Boil the spaghetti.
  2. Cook the beef with garlic and onion.
  3. Add the tomato sauce.
  4. Mix together and serve.
